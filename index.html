About SAPience

Foundational years, taken seriously — and joyfully.

Run under Sapience Academy and Management Solution since 2022, SAPience International Kids School builds its classrooms around play, not worksheets. Discussion, role‑play and hands‑on activity carry the academic groundwork for children aged two to six.

Academic Programs

One staircase, four steps

Every child climbs the same four blocks at their own pace — each stage building directly on what the last one taught.

Step 1

Playgroup

First separation from home, first friendships, first routines — through songs, sensory play and gentle structure.

Step 2

Nursery

Vocabulary, numbers and shapes enter through stories and games, not repetition.

Step 3

LKG

Reading readiness, phonics and early writing, paired with more independent activity time.

Step 4

UKG

School‑ready confidence: fluent conversation, structured tasks, and stage presence built through SAP Day rehearsals.

Beyond the Syllabus

Skills we build on purpose

English fluency & phonics

Conversation practice and phonics work that gets children speaking with ease, not just reciting.

Activity & creative arts

Craft, art and hands‑on activity blocks that turn each lesson into something a child can hold and make.

Public speaking & stage confidence

Guided practice in front of an audience — the same skill that carries a four‑year‑old onto the SAP Day stage.

Play‑based & role‑play learning

Discussion and role‑play used as teaching tools, so social and cognitive skills grow side by side.

Annual Showcases

SAP Day, and every stage in between

Once a year, the classroom becomes a stage. SAP Day brings together dance performances and cultural celebrations that children rehearse for all year — the same public‑speaking and stage practice that runs through daily lessons, on full display for families.
