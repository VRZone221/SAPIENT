Our Story

Founded in 2022, built on play.

SAPience International Kids School is run under Sapience Academy and Management Solution, and is recognised by the Government of Karnataka. From day one, the idea was simple: young children learn best through play, conversation and hands-on activity — not worksheets and rote memorisation.

Our Approach

What we believe about early learning

Three ideas guide every lesson plan, every activity block, and every SAP Day rehearsal.

Play is the curriculum

Songs, role-play and sensory activity carry academic groundwork — children absorb concepts by doing, not repeating.

Confidence, spoken aloud

English fluency and public speaking are practiced daily, so stage confidence isn't a once-a-year skill — it's a habit.

Every stage builds the next

Playgroup, Nursery, LKG and UKG are designed as one staircase, so nothing a child learns is ever wasted.
